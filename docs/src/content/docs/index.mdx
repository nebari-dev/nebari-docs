  <div class="nebari-split">
    <div>
      <h2 id="what-you-can-do">What can you do with Nebari?</h2>
      <p class="nebari-section__subtitle">Nebari runs the AI and data science tools your team needs on infrastructure you control.</p>
    </div>

    <ul class="nebari-rows" role="list">
      <FeatureRow icon="cloud">
        <h3>Run AI on infrastructure you own</h3>
        <p>Deploy into your own cloud account, or onto your own hardware. Your models, data, and prompts stay where you already keep them.</p>
        <a href="/docs/how-tos/providers/">Pick a provider</a>
      </FeatureRow>
      <FeatureRow icon="rocket">
        <h3>Go from prototype to something your team uses</h3>
        <p>Package what you built as a Software Pack. It comes up with its own address, a login, and routing already wired in.</p>
        <a href="/docs/explanations/software-packs/">How packs work</a>
      </FeatureRow>
      <FeatureRow icon="package">
        <h3>Add capabilities as you need them</h3>
        <p>Install only the packs you need today. Add serving, chat, or agent workflows later, and upgrade each one on its own.</p>
        <a href="https://packs.nebari.dev/">Browse the catalog</a>
      </FeatureRow>
      <FeatureRow icon="git-branch">
        <h3>Give every teammate the same setup</h3>
        <p>Everything on the platform is declared in a git repository and reconciled from it, so the cluster your teammate opens is the one you described.</p>
        <a href="/docs/explanations/nkp-architecture/#gitops-via-argocd">GitOps on Nebari</a>
      </FeatureRow>
      <FeatureRow icon="activity">
        <h3>Watch what your AI is doing</h3>
        <p>Logs, metrics, and traces are collected from every running pack and forwarded to the observability backend of your choice.</p>
        <a href="/docs/explanations/nkp-architecture/#how-the-layers-fit-together">Platform architecture</a>
      </FeatureRow>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>

          <td data-label="With Nebari">

        <tr data-draw>

          <td data-label="Without Nebari"><span class="nebari-chore">Build a login for every internal app.</span></td>

            <a href="/docs/how-tos/keycloak-auth/">Set up single sign-on</a>

        <tr data-draw>

<section class="nebari-section nebari-section--muted nebari-principles" aria-labelledby="principles">
  <h2 id="principles">Development principles</h2>

  <ul class="nebari-principles__list not-content" role="list">
    <li>
      <h3>Open source</h3>
      <p>All projects are developed under an open source license, and built using open source software.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Community-oriented</h3>
      <p>All projects welcome community contributions and engagement in project spaces and community meetings.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <h3>Composable architecture</h3>
      <p>All projects have a modular design and integrate with each other, while also allowing for independent use and extensibility.</p>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
