<section class="nebari-section" aria-labelledby="what-you-can-do">
  <h2 id="what-you-can-do">What can you do with Nebari?</h2>
  <p class="nebari-section__subtitle">Nebari runs the AI and data science tools your team needs on infrastructure you control.</p>

  <div class="nebari-rows nebari-rows--split">
    <FeatureRow icon="cloud">
      <h3>Run AI on infrastructure you own</h3>
      <p>Deploy into your own cloud account, or onto your own hardware. Your models, data, and prompts stay where you already keep them.</p>
      <a href="/docs/how-tos/providers/">Pick a provider</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="rocket">
      <h3>Go from prototype to something your team uses</h3>
      <p>Package what you built as a Software Pack. It comes up with its own address, a login, and routing already wired in.</p>
      <a href="/docs/explanations/software-packs/">How packs work</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="blocks">
      <h3>Add capabilities as you need them</h3>
      <p>Install only the packs you need today. Add serving, chat, or agent workflows later, and upgrade each one on its own.</p>
      <a href="https://packs.nebari.dev/">Browse the catalog</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="branch">
      <h3>Give every teammate the same setup</h3>
      <p>Everything on the platform is declared in a git repository and reconciled from it, so the cluster your teammate opens is the one you described.</p>
      <a href="/docs/explanations/nkp-architecture/#gitops-via-argocd">GitOps on Nebari</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="pulse">
      <h3>Watch what your AI is doing</h3>
      <p>Logs, metrics, and traces are collected from every running pack and forwarded to the observability backend of your choice.</p>
      <a href="/docs/explanations/nkp-architecture/#how-the-layers-fit-together">Platform architecture</a>
    </FeatureRow>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="nebari-section" aria-labelledby="what-you-dont-have-to-do">
  <h2 id="what-you-dont-have-to-do">What you don't have to do</h2>
  <p class="nebari-section__subtitle">Nebari runs the layer underneath your apps, so your team doesn't have to.</p>

  <div class="nebari-rows nebari-rows--two">
    <FeatureRow icon="clock">
      <h3>Wait on someone else to deploy it</h3>
      <p>Install a pack and it comes up on its own address, behind the login your company already uses.</p>
      <a href="/docs/software-packs/">Software Packs</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="lock">
      <h3>Build a login for every internal app</h3>
      <p>Colleagues sign in once with the account they already have, and you decide which groups reach which app.</p>
      <a href="/docs/how-tos/keycloak-auth/">Set up single sign-on</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="chip">
      <h3>Rent a GPU box on the side</h3>
      <p>GPU machines are a node group in the same cluster, so your team uses the hardware it already pays for.</p>
      <a href="/docs/how-tos/update-cluster/">Change a running cluster</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="database">
      <h3>Back up your own work</h3>
      <p>Turn backups on once, and the platform copies your notebooks and files to off-cluster storage every day.</p>
      <a href="/docs/how-tos/backup-restore/">Back up and restore</a>
    </FeatureRow>
    <FeatureRow icon="layers">
      <h3>Learn Kubernetes to ship your project</h3>
      <p>The platform handles what runs underneath. You work with notebooks, apps, and models.</p>
      <a href="/docs/introduction/">What Nebari is</a>
    </FeatureRow>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="nebari-section nbr-grid-3" aria-labelledby="ecosystem">
  <h2 id="ecosystem">Ecosystem of tools</h2>
  <p class="nebari-section__subtitle">Pick the pieces you need. Every project is open source and designed to work on its own or together.</p>

  <CardGrid>
    <Card title="Nebari core" icon="rocket">
      New robust core infrastructure layer for platform deployment on popular cloud providers. Security, authentication,
      role-based access, and observability built in by design.

      [Install NIC](/docs/get-started/install/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Nebi" icon="seti:git">
      Tools to manage, share, and collaborate on the AI software lifecycle across your team. Includes future plans for
      SBoM generation and compliance checking.

      [Nebi on GitHub](https://github.com/nebari-dev/nebi)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Usage-specific UIs" icon="laptop">
      Multiple UI options available: Jupyter/VSCode for developers, chat and dashboard interfaces for AI users, and more.

      [Software packs dashboard](https://packs.nebari.dev/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="AI model serving pack" icon="cloud-download">
      Serve and monitor different AI models for your development workflow or production applications.

      [LLM serving pack docs](https://packs.nebari.dev/llm-serving-pack/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Chat assistant pack" icon="comment">
      A ready-to-use chat interface for your teams, wired into platform login and connected to the models you serve on
      Nebari.

      [Chat pack docs](https://packs.nebari.dev/chat-pack/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Agentic AI pack" icon="puzzle">
      Frameworks for building, deploying, evaluating, and monitoring AI agent-based workflows.

      [Pi coding agent docs](https://packs.nebari.dev/pi-coding-agent-pack/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Data orchestration pack" icon="random">
      Tools for building data engineering pipelines, and governance and provenance of data and AI assets.

      <Badge text="Coming soon" />
    </Card>
    <Card title="Custom integrations" icon="setting">
      All Nebari projects are developed on an open and extensible architecture that allows for quick integration of any
      libraries you need.

      [Build your own pack](/docs/software-packs/build-your-own/)
    </Card>
    <Card title="Community hub" icon="heart">
      A space for community members to share their custom packs.

      [Join the community](/community/introduction/)
    </Card>
  </CardGrid>
</section>
