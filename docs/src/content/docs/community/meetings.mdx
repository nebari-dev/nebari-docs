Community meetings are the easiest way to meet the people building Nebari, ask questions, and hear what's coming next.
Everyone is welcome. Users, contributors, and anyone curious about the project are invited to join.

We have a:

- bi-weekly 1-hour community meeting to chat about everything Nebari, and
- weekly 30-minute triage meeting where we triage and follow up on open issues and PRs.

## Past meeting notes

Notes from previous meetings are posted in the Nebari GitHub Discussions.

[Read past community meeting notes →](https://github.com/nebari-dev/nebari/discussions/categories/community-meetings)
